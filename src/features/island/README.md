# Island

What: **My World**, the whole screen at `/world`: a knitted island that grows as the rider contributes on the map, with a small gift that can wait on it. `/island`, `/rewards` and `/my-challenges` redirect here.
Owner: Rachel. Only the owner edits this folder.

## How it fits together
- `Island.tsx`: the page. Full screen under the nav (like the map). Reads the rider's contribution count with `stampCount(useMyProgress())` from `features/explore/progress`. UI is only a title, one contextual knit button, one small status (with Retry) and a help button (`IslandHelp.tsx`: tips, sound, preview, credit). No counters, balances, feeds or tabs.
- `content.ts`: all text and `unlockSteps`: the journey, 12 steps, one per contribution (a bare quilt, river and yarn ball at 0; grass and flowers, a tree, pines, stones, the cottage, garden and fence, a sheep, more trees, the flock, the bridge and river life, kite and laundry, clouds). Each step lists how many pieces of each scene stage exist (`allowedFor`, `isIn`). Only existing pieces are used; more contributions are still recorded by the server but add nothing yet.
- `IslandLoader.tsx` / `IslandScene.tsx`: load the three.js scene in the browser only, and clean it up when you leave.
- `yarn-world/`: **Yarn World by Kris Yang** (https://github.com/krisyangdev/yarn-world, MIT, see `yarn-world/LICENSE-yarn-world`).
  Our changes: `mount.js` (replaces `main.js`: sized to its container, cleaned up on leave), `weave.js` + `world.js` (`isUnlocked` keeps locked pieces hidden; `scrub` makes the pull drive the knit-in; palette `P` retuned to warm stone, ivory, sage and sandstone with MBTA accents),
  `guide.js`, `sound.js`, `ambience.js` (mount and dispose). Sounds are CC0, in `public/assets/island/sfx/`.

## Opening: pull the thread
- Drag the loose thread (mouse or touch). The distance you drag sets how far the knit-in has gone (about half the window height = the whole island). It only moves forward; let go and the rest knits by itself.
- "Knit the island for me" starts the same knit-in without dragging (keyboard and screen-reader alternative; progress is announced).
- Code: `scrub` in `yarn-world/weave.js` (`pullSpan` is set from the camera distance in `mount.js`).

## The journey: Explore -> stamp -> island
- **Source of truth:** the server. A completed report or verification is stored by `/api/submissions` and `/api/verifications` (one row per rider per quest, so no duplicates). Stamps = `stampCount(useMyProgress())` in `features/explore/progress.ts`. Nothing on the island awards or counts points.
- **Explore:** after a successful submission `Celebration` shows the server's `tokensAwarded`, the stamp, and which island piece the new stamp unlocks. Two buttons: "Continue exploring" (stays on the map, nothing moves) and "Visit my world" (`/world`). Reopening a finished quest shows "Already in your collection" with no fanfare.
- **Map memory:** `features/explore/viewMemory.ts` keeps the camera and selected stop for the browser tab (sessionStorage). Next keeps pages mounted but hidden (`cacheComponents`), and a hidden map loses its camera, so `ExploreMap` puts it back when the page is shown again.
- **Island visits:** `seen.ts` remembers how many journey steps this browser has seen (display memory only). First visit: the yarn-pull opening, with every earned step knitted in. Later visits: the island is simply there, and each step earned since the last visit **drops onto it from above** (`dropIn` in `weave.js`; `world.dropIn` / `mount.js`), one after another, a beat after the page opens. Under reduced motion the pieces just appear. A step is remembered only after its pieces have landed. If the server cannot be reached, the island shows what this browser last saw and adds nothing.
- Every visit re-fetches stamps (`refreshProgress`) and re-plans, because the page stays mounted between visits.

## Notes
- The yarn ball, the quilt and the river are always there. The unlock set is read when the thread is pulled.
- "Preview the full island" shows everything without touching real stamps (good for the demo).
- Keys: `M` mutes. Sound starts after the first touch on the scene (browsers require it).
- The scene is about 480 KB of JavaScript, loaded only on `/world`.

## The collection gallery ("Unlock more of your island")
A small link under the title opens a gallery that floats above the (dimmed) island: a heading, one row of the island's own knitted pieces, one detail line and one progress bar. Nothing else: no checklist, tokens, cards or offers (gifts stay in the 3D world).
- **The pieces are the island's real objects.** `world.collectibles()` lists, for each of the 12 journey steps (same order as `unlockSteps`), the existing objects that stand for it (flower cluster, round tree, pines, stones, cottage, garden and fence, sheep, bridge, a pinwheel, clouds...). `yarn-world/collection.js` CLONES them into its own scene (same geometry, textures and wool materials, including the fuzz shader), so they look exactly like the island's. The island's scene, renderer and camera are never touched.
- **One extra renderer, not twelve.** One transparent canvas, one scene and one camera draw the whole row; it exists only while the gallery is open and is disposed on close. Every piece is normalised to the same size and centred (flat things get a little more), with soft ambient light and a soft shadow, no card, pedestal or circle.
- **Motion:** each piece floats about 6 px and turns slowly with its own phase; hovering lifts it a little, scales it up and almost stops its turn; the selected piece moves slightly forward. Reduced Motion turns the floating, turning and sliding off. A piece earned since this browser last looked rises into view once (`readCollectionSeen` / `writeCollectionSeen` in `seen.ts`) and is not replayed.
- **Locked pieces** use a muted copy of the same materials (softer colour, a little see-through, keeps the fuzz shader) with a lock by the label; unlocked pieces use the original materials with a tick.
- **Real progress only.** Unlocked is `index < known`, where `known` is the server-confirmed count the island already uses. If progress cannot be loaded nothing is shown as unlocked, there is no bar, and it says so; no fake unlocks. The detail line for a locked piece gives its real requirement ("N more contributions to go").
- **Use it:** mouse (hover, click, drag, wheel or trackpad scroll: the row follows the pointer, then glides to the nearest piece; no side buttons), touch (drag; one prominent piece with its neighbours peeking in on a phone, 5 pieces on desktop), keyboard (Left/Right, Home/End, Esc; the labels are buttons with a roving tab stop). Opening holds the island camera still (nothing moves or resets it) and closing returns focus to the link; the camera stays exactly as it was.

## Things to explore on the island
Three small knitted objects on the near side of the island, each with one purpose. No cards float over the scene; the clouds are just clouds.
- **Journal** (`yarn-world/journal.js`): your history. **Two gift boxes** (`yarn-world/parcel.js`): the red-ribbon box is a local treat and opens a **voucher**; the blue-ribbon box is **"Unlock more of your island"** and opens the collection gallery (the floating row of 3D pieces and the progress bar). The old locked parcels and the header link are gone: the box is the way in. After the gallery closes the box's lid closes again. Reduced Motion turns the wiggle and drift off.
- **The voucher** (`Voucher.tsx`, `VoucherArt.tsx`, `GiftTicket.tsx`): the treat gift opens into a narrow paper voucher that unfolds out of the gift part by part (picture, name, offer, then the stub), with a dashed tear line and a zig-zag receipt edge (it just appears under Reduced Motion). The picture is a real Google photo when there is one, otherwise a little illustration of the KIND of place (a cup, a croissant, a plate); it never shows a specific shop. Three kinds, never mixed up: a REAL nearby place (name, category, address, distance, "View on Map", no offer), a DEMO offer (made-up merchant, stamped "Demo — Not Redeemable", example code that works nowhere) and a folded, locked voucher. The journey path now lives in the collection gallery, not on the voucher; the voucher only says how far the next gift is and links to the gallery.
- **First-visit tour** (`Tour.tsx`, copy in `content.tour`, remembered once in `seen.ts` as display memory): after the island is knitted the camera zooms in and a card at the top walks through five steps: welcome, **My Journey** (the journal), **Your gifts**, **Still to explore** (the unlock box), then **Make it grow** with an "Open the map" button. The rider sets the pace: scroll (the wheel steps through the tour instead of zooming the scene), swipe the card, arrow keys / Page keys, the dots, or Back / Next. The camera flies to each part as the step changes (`zoomIn`, `focusHotspot`, `resetCamera`) and the part glows. "Skip tour" / Esc ends it; opening a ticket or the gallery pauses it. Not shown in the preview; "Take the tour again" is in the help panel. The gifts and journal are drawn 1.8x so a first-time rider notices them.
- **Telling riders what unlocks more:** locked gifts hang faint with a "Locked" tag; the gift and journal tickets show "Next on your island: <next piece>", "Next gift after N more contributions", the full 12-step path with Gift 1/2/3 marked, and a "Contribute on the map" button; after a contribution the map says what lands on the island next time.
- **Hover, focus or select** an object: it glows, and one compact glass label (`HotspotLabel.tsx`) appears above it ("A free cookie · Example gift · tap to open", "New discovery · 2 more contributions to open", "Your journal"). Only one at a time, never all. The label follows the object's real screen position, stays inside the screen edges and hides if the object is off screen. Keyboard users get hidden buttons (they show on focus) that also highlight the object.
- **Click or tap** an object: a small ticket opens beside it (it closes with the X, a "Back to my world" button, Esc, or a tap on the island outside it; the collection gallery has the same button) (`TicketFrame.tsx` picks above, below or beside so it does not cover the object, shrinks and scrolls if there is no room). Esc or the close button returns to the same view; nothing navigates.
  - **Journal ticket** (`JournalTicket.tsx`): the rider's real reports and checks from `/api/submissions`, with the status each really has (draft, coach has suggestions, passed the check but not finished, completed in Tada on a date, "MBTA has since marked this fixed", you said it works / is still broken). If history cannot load it says so with a Retry; it never presents a failed load as an empty journal. Finishing a mission here is not an MBTA report, and it says so.
  - **Gift ticket** (`GiftTicket.tsx`): the offer on one side, your journey on the other (every step, ticks for what is on the island, Gift 1/2/3 where they open, "Next gift after N more contributions"). Locked gifts say how many more contributions open them.
- **Camera:** `focusHotspot(id)` / `resetCamera()` (`mount.js`) fly it smoothly to an object or back (time-based, instant under Reduced Motion, cancelled by a drag). The floating tags, tips and labels share one cream "liquid glass" look (`glass.ts`: not fully see-through, soft blur, bright rim, sheen) and sit well above their object so they never cover it. It sways gently (about 29 degrees each way) instead of spinning, so the near-side objects never turn out of view; it pauses while you drag, while a pointer is on an object and while a ticket is open. It sits a little closer on wide screens and looks down more steeply on tall (phone) screens so the island fills the screen.
- **Real nearby places.** Each unlocked gift can show a real café, bakery or restaurant near the rider's selected MBTA stop (`usePlaces.ts` -> `GET /api/places/nearby?stationId=`). The stop comes from the map (`selectedStationId()` in `explore/viewMemory.ts`: this tab's stop, else the last stop picked, saved in localStorage as a public station id). Only the station's public coordinates are ever used; the rider's own location is never asked for here or sent anywhere. With no stop picked, or if the lookup fails, the gift shows the labelled demo instead and says why; the island never waits on it.
  - **Default source, no key, no cost: an OpenStreetMap snapshot** (`src/lib/data/nearby-places.json`, built by `node scripts/build-nearby-places.mjs` from the public Overpass API). OpenStreetMap data is open (ODbL), so it may be stored, and the ticket credits "© OpenStreetMap contributors". Independent (non-chain) places are preferred, then a café, a bakery and a restaurant so the three gifts differ. It has **no photos** (the ticket shows a plain placeholder), and businesses open and close, so re-run the script to refresh (the file records its build date).
  - **Optional upgrade: Google Places (adds a real photo).** If `GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY` is set (Places API (New) enabled; see `.env.example`), the route asks Google live and the photo goes through `GET /api/places/photo` so the key stays on the server; if Google fails it falls back to the snapshot. Google's terms forbid caching or storing place content (only the place id), require the photo author credit and forbid drawing their data on a non-Google map, so nothing from Google is stored and we only link out. Cost: Nearby Search with these fields is Google's Pro tier (about $32 per 1,000 requests after about 5,000 free a month; photos about $7 per 1,000 after about 1,000 free; confirm on Google's pricing page), and every open is a request, so set a budget and quota cap.
  - Real from the source: name, category, address, map link (and, with Google, the photo). Computed by us from real coordinates: the distance. **Not provided and never invented: any offer, discount, code or partnership.** A place ticket has no offer fields and says so.
  - Content kinds stay separate in `gifts.ts`: `place` (real business, no offer), `demo` (fictional, "Demo — Not Redeemable", example code `EXAMPLE-ONLY-n`), and the reserved `real-offer` / `verified-promotion`, which nothing produces yet.
- **There is no real offer source yet.** `gifts.ts` holds three clearly labelled demos. "Reveal the code" shows an obvious example code (`EXAMPLE-ONLY-n`) with "works nowhere"; no real code is invented and no email is asked. "Opened" is remembered in the browser only (`seen.ts`), fine because demos have no value. A genuine offer must come from the server (an issued entitlement, claim and redemption state in the database, server-enforced expiry, abuse prevention) and `Gift` already has the fields; real and demo offers are told apart by `demo`.
- "Preview the full island" (help button) shows every gift ready.

## Behind the scenes (not shown on this page)
Submissions, verifications, token balance and like stamps are still recorded and returned by `/api/submissions`, `/api/community` and `/api/likes`. My World does not show them.
